{\songti\fontsize{8.5}{13}\selectfont 随着集成电路规模与设计复杂度持续提升，时序收敛已成为数字芯片设计流程中的关键挑战。工程变更命令（engineering change order，ECO）通过在已有设计上做局部修改，可降低重新综合与物理实现带来的迭代成本。其中，预布局门级时序 ECO 在布局布线前探索局部网表修改方案，但受候选空间限制、候选级评估开销较高。为此提出候选探索与失效刻画框架 FAECO：在违例路径局部逻辑上以多特征加权割与关键路径覆盖割生成并排序四类局部修改候选——逻辑等价替换（R）、门尺寸调整（G）、缓冲器插入（B）与联合修改（JOINT）；将候选失效刻画为六类，作为搜索诊断与消融分析的依据；候选按轮提交，依次经函数与结构检查、时序分析与最终等价性检查。在 ISCAS89、ITC-99 和 PicoRV32 上，8/8、18/19 和 2/2 个实例取得预布局理想线网 WNS 改善（PicoRV32 第三个子模块无 setup 路径，不参与改善判定），其余无退化。固定权重、20 轮迭代下，混合候选平均改善 1.07\,ns，高于仅门尺寸调整的 0.16\,ns。消融实验进一步分析候选反馈机制与结构重综合候选对搜索效果的影响；实验结果表明，候选空间扩展与候选排序是预布局 ECO 搜索效果的重要影响因素。}

[EN]
\small Timing closure has become a critical challenge in digital chip design as circuit scale and design complexity continue to grow. Engineering change order (ECO) techniques reduce the iteration cost of re-synthesis and physical implementation by applying localized modifications to an existing design. Among them, pre-placement gate-level timing ECO explores local netlist modifications before placement and routing, but its candidate space is limited and per-candidate evaluation is costly. FAECO is a framework for candidate exploration and failure characterization. It builds and ranks four types of local modification candidates — logic-equivalent replacement (R), gate sizing (G), buffer insertion (B), and joint modification (JOINT) — over the local logic around violating paths via a multi-feature weighted cut and a critical-path cover cut, characterizes candidate failures into six types used for search diagnosis and ablation analysis, and commits candidates round by round through function and structural checks, timing analysis and final equivalence checking. On ISCAS89, ITC-99 and PicoRV32, 8/8, 18/19 and 2/2 instances achieve pre-placement ideal-net WNS improvement (the third PicoRV32 module has no setup path and is excluded), with no regression on the rest. Under fixed weights and 20 rounds, mixed candidates improve WNS by 1.07\,ns on average versus 0.16\,ns for gate sizing alone. Ablations further analyze the effects of the failure-feedback mechanism and structure-resynthesis candidates on search performance. These results highlight the importance of candidate exploration and ranking in pre-placement ECO search.
