# AI-use disclosure working record

This is an author-review draft. It does not certify that every author has reviewed or approved any output. The known work in this Codex project extends beyond language editing.

| Use | Known scope | Required author confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| Research software | Code inspection, implementation assistance and test construction, including candidate-STA accounting and submission-state semantics | Which contributions entered the experimental software; tool/model/version, date ranges, human verification and reproducibility controls |
| Evidence and analysis | Review of archived configurations, candidate records, report crosswalks, measurement counts and interpretations; identification of confounding and failure cases | Full historical tool inventory and exact review boundaries; which numerical summaries were independently checked by authors |
| Scientific figures | Scripted vector workflow diagrams; adaptation of Python plotting code; translation of figure labels; plots computed from existing experimental evidence | Exact model/tool/version and developer; author validation of every data transform, caption and schematic relationship |
| Manuscript preparation | Drafting/revision assistance, review simulation, Chinese-to-English translation, Elsevier-format preparation and compilation/layout checks | Complete inventory of tools used across earlier writing rounds and author responsibility/review statement |

The English draft therefore includes two distinct records: a Methods subsection for research and reproducible figure assistance, and a separate manuscript-preparation declaration. Figures 1–2 identify OpenAI Codex assistance in their captions and retain a conspicuous model/version placeholder. Quantitative figures reuse archived real observations and reproducible Python computations; they do not introduce generated measurements.

**AUTHOR INPUT REQUIRED:** exact model names and versions have not been recovered for all historical sessions. Do not replace this with “AI was used only for language polishing.” Do not sign an assertion that all authors reviewed the work until that review has occurred. If the historical inventory is incomplete, describe the known scope and the remaining uncertainty honestly.

The publisher's current requirements were checked against the official [Elsevier AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/en-gb/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals) on 14 October 2026; see `GUIDE_VERIFICATION.md` for the journal-guide limitation.
